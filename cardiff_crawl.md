# The Cardiff 9-Hole Craft Beer Crawl (Canton to City Centre)

A rain-proof, winter-friendly indoor pub crawl designed to move from Canton, near Victoria Park, into the heart of Cardiff city centre.

## The 9-Hole Route

- **Hole 1: The Lansdowne Pub**
  - **Location:** 71 Beda Rd, Canton, CF5 1LX
  - **Vibe:** Traditional neighbourhood craft pub with well-kept cask lines.
  - **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Restaurant_Review-g186460-d7219688-Reviews-The_Lansdowne_Pub-Cardiff_South_Wales_Wales.html)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 6 minutes

- **Hole 2: The Pig & Swill**
  - **Location:** 1 Victoria Park Road West, Canton, CF5 1EZ
  - **Vibe:** Boutique neighbourhood gastro-taproom with excellent independent craft lines.
  - **Links:** [Google Reviews](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Pig+%26+Swill+Cardiff)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 10 minutes

- **Hole 3: Crafty Devil's Cellar**
  - **Location:** 16 Llandaff Rd, Canton, CF11 9NJ
  - **Vibe:** Snug, independent local brewery taproom and bottle shop.
  - **Links:** [Google Reviews](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Crafty+Devil%27s+Cellar+Cardiff)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 7 minutes

- **Hole 4: The Corporation**
  - **Location:** 188 Cowbridge Rd E, Canton, CF5 1GW
  - **Vibe:** Historic market hall with local street food stalls and a strong central bar.
  - **Links:** [Google Reviews](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Corporation+Cardiff)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 11 minutes *(crosses the River Taff into the city centre)*

- **Hole 5: Mad Dog Brewery Co**
  - **Location:** 17-19 Castle St, City Centre, CF10 1BS
  - **Vibe:** Cozy, independent Welsh brewery taproom serving beers made with water from the Brecon Beacons.
  - **Links:** [Google Reviews](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mad+Dog+Brewery+Co+Cardiff)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 4 minutes

- **Hole 6: Tiny Rebel Cardiff**
  - **Location:** 25 Westgate St, City Centre, CF10 1DD
  - **Vibe:** High-energy multi-room venue with iconic Welsh craft beer and arcade games.
  - **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Restaurant_Review-g186460-d4890740-Reviews-Tiny_Rebel_Cardiff-Cardiff_South_Wales_Wales.html)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 1 minute *(just around the corner)*

- **Hole 7: The City Arms**
  - **Location:** 10-12 Quay St, City Centre, CF10 1DY
  - **Vibe:** Historic Victorian pub known for a large rotation of real ale.
  - **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Attraction_Review-g186460-d10064754-Reviews-The_City_Arms-Cardiff_South_Wales_Wales.html)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 3 minutes

- **Hole 8: The Head of Steam Cardiff**
  - **Location:** 18-19 Church St, City Centre, CF10 1BG
  - **Vibe:** Large two-storey beer hall with craft beer, world imports, and shuffleboard.
  - **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Restaurant_Review-g186460-d15238289-Reviews-The_Head_of_Steam_Cardiff-Cardiff_South_Wales_Wales.html)
  - **Walk to next stop:** Approx. 2 minutes

- **Hole 9: Cambrian Tap**
  - **Location:** 51 St Mary St, City Centre, CF10 1AD
  - **Vibe:** Rustic-industrial craft beer finale with up to 18 local and guest draft lines.
  - **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Restaurant_Review-g186460-d29702700-Reviews-The_Cambrian_Tap_Cardiff-Cardiff_South_Wales_Wales.html)
  - **Finish:** Open late until 1:30 AM on weekends
