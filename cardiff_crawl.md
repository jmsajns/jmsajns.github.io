# 🍺 The Cardiff 9-Hole Craft Beer Crawl (Canton to City Centre)

A rain-proof, winter-friendly indoor pub crawl designed to move systematically from Canton (near Victoria Park) straight into the heart of Cardiff City Centre. 

## 📋 The 9-Hole Master List & Walking Times

*   **Hole 1: The Lansdowne Pub**
    *   **Location:** 71 Beda Rd, Canton, CF5 1LX
    *   **Vibe:** Traditional neighborhood craft local with immaculately kept cask lines.
    *   **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://tripadvisor.co.uk)
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **6 minutes**

*   **Hole 2: The Pig & Swill**
    *   **Location:** 1 Victoria Park Road West, Canton, CF5 1EZ
    *   **Vibe:** Trendy, boutique neighborhood gastro-taproom featuring excellent independent craft lines.
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **10 minutes**

*   **Hole 3: Crafty Devil's Cellar**
    *   **Location:** 16 Llandaff Rd, Canton, CF11 9NJ
    *   **Vibe:** Super snug, fiercely independent local brewery taproom and bottle shop.
    *   **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://tripadvisor.co.uk)
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **7 minutes**

*   **Hole 4: The Corporation**
    *   **Location:** 188 Cowbridge Rd E, Canton, CF5 1GW
    *   **Vibe:** Striking historic market hall featuring localized street food stalls and a great central bar.
    *   **Links:** [Tripadvisor Profile](https://tripadvisor.co.uk)
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **11 minutes** *(Crosses the river Taff into the City Centre)*

*   **Hole 5: Mad Dog Brewery Co**
    *   **Location:** 17-19 Castle St, City Centre, CF10 1BS
    *   **Vibe:** Cozy, independent Welsh brewery taproom serving beers made with Brecon Beacons water.
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **4 minutes**

*   **Hole 6: Tiny Rebel Cardiff**
    *   **Location:** 25 Westgate St, City Centre, CF10 1DD
    *   **Vibe:** High-energy, multi-room flagship venue for iconic Welsh craft beer and arcade games.
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **1 minute** *(Just around the corner)*

*   **Hole 7: The City Arms**
    *   **Location:** 10-12 Quay St, City Centre, CF10 1DY
    *   **Vibe:** Legendary historic 1880s Victorian institution famous for its massive real ale rotation.
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **3 minutes**

*   **Hole 8: The Head of Steam Cardiff**
    *   **Location:** 18-19 Church St, City Centre, CF10 1BG
    *   **Vibe:** Expansive two-story indoor beer palace showcasing craft beer, world imports, and shuffleboard.
    *   🚶 **Walk to next stop:** Approx. **2 minutes**

*   **Hole 9: Cambrian Tap**
    *   **Location:** 51 St Mary St, City Centre, CF10 1AD
    *   **Vibe:** Rustic-industrial craft beer finale featuring up to 18 local and guest draft lines.
    *   🎉 **Finish Line!** (Open late until 1:30 AM on weekends)
